# v1.2: E, emotion and prosody (2026-11-23)

The owner's problem was that the voice sounded "very monotone, robotic", while the YouTube original has ups and downs. Each dub line is synthesized up to 30 s **before** its original audio plays. So the per-line emotion comes from **text cues known ahead of time**. The **scene energy** comes from the recent past of the original audio; it changes slowly, so lagging behind is fine.

## Measurements
Setup: Playwright Chromium, through the extension (popup page → SW → offscreen → worker, 4 threads). The scene arousal was set to 0.75 (MrBeast-like). The F0 statistics come from voiced 5 ms frames: the range is p95−p5 in semitones, and std is the semitone standard deviation. The WAVs are in `research/samples/v12/` and the raw numbers in `measurements.json`.

| Line (Siwis unless noted) | Style | F0 range st, Expr 0 → 0.7 | F0 std st, 0 → 0.7 | Mean F0 Hz | Duration ms | RTF at 0.7 (incl. post) | Post ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Aujourd'hui, on va construire une maison…" | neutral | 7.8 → 9.4 | 2.59 → 3.30 | 230 → 234 | 3173 → 3293 | 0.090 | 52 |
| "Incroyable ! Il a gagné dix mille dollars !" | excited | 10.2 → 19.0 | 3.13 → 6.12 | 185 → 230 | 2462 → 2675 | 0.077 | 44 |
| "Est-ce que tu veux vraiment garder tout cet argent ?" | question | 6.7 → 10.0 | 2.02 → 3.24 | 199 → 223 | 2100 → 2260 | 0.079 | 43 |
| "Le dernier à quitter le cercle gagne 500 000 dollars !" (caps) | shout | 10.2 → 13.5 | 2.90 → 4.68 | 218 → 277 | 2960 → 3284 | 0.114 | 59 |
| Tom, excited line | excited | 5.8 → 10.6 | 1.64 → 3.30 | 136 → 164 | 3038 → 3032 | 0.128 | 48 |

- **RTF after post-processing: 0.077–0.128, which is under the 0.3 target.** Post-processing costs 43–90 ms per line.
- Peaks are ≤ 0.95, so there is no clipping. Durations are exact, because PSOLA preserves length.
- The F0 std rises ×1.3 (neutral) to ×2 (excited/Tom).
- Unit tests check that PSOLA shifts F0 by +4/−3/+2.5/+7 st within ±0.5 st with the same length, and that ratio 1 is close to identity. They also check that k = 1.6 raises the std by more than 1.3×, that a question rises at the end only, that +4 dB plus a +6 dB emphasis stays ≤ 0.95, that an emphasis bump changes only its own span, and that a no-op ProsodyParams gives identical WAV bytes.

## Open issues
1. **Not yet heard by a human.** The numbers look right, but the owner should listen to `research/samples/v12/*-expr0.wav` vs `*-expr070.wav`. PSOLA on real speech can add slight roughness at k ≥ 1.6 (excited std doubles). If it sounds cartoonish, lower `STYLE_BASE.excited.k` / `shout.k` in `shared/prosody.ts`, or tell kids to use ~50 %.
2. **Siwis defaults are noise_scale 0.5 / noise_w 0.333** (Tom: 0.667/0.8). The requested ranges (0.55–0.9 / 0.6–1.1) are therefore livelier than stock Siwis even for "neutral". They also make Siwis lines about **5 % longer** on average, with more run-to-run tempo variation (measured: 3150 → 3300 ms median over 5 runs). `knownMs` is exact and the planner absorbs it, but rate pressure on dense clusters goes up a little. A per-model relative mapping would avoid this.
3. Word timing for emphasis comes from character position on the speech-active timeline, because the Piper ONNX exposes no durations. That is good enough for one or two stressed words, but not exact.
4. Gemini Nano tagging is untested in real Chrome: the same unknowns as F (isolated vs MAIN world `LanguageModel`, latency). It never blocks, and the text rules carry the feature on their own.
5. The scene arousal needs the WebAudio tap. If `AudioContext` fails, or the official audio track is used, it stays at 0.5. Prefetched neural lines (≤ 30 s ahead) use the arousal at prepare time. That is acceptable because arousal is slow by design.
6. The cache key bump (`:3`) means the first replay of a v1.1-cached video re-fetches its captions once.
7. `popup-screenshots.spec.ts` regenerates `screenshots/` from `dist` (`EXT_DIR`). The lead may want to refresh them from the final build so that they show the Expression slider.
