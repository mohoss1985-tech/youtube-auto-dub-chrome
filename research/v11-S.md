# v1.1 — S: smooth pacing + smooth speaker switching (2026-11-23)

Owner feedback (v1.0): (1) "silences, then they speak fast to catch up"; (2) "male/female switching is not smooth".

## Root causes found
1. **The silence holes come from a v1.0 session bug.** `DubSession.speak()` dropped every line that was more than 0.3 s late when it reached the engine (strict-mode logic). But continuous mode starts lines late on purpose, chained after the previous line. Each overrun line therefore silenced the next one. Then the line after that got the per-line catch-up boost (up to ×1.35). That is exactly what the owner heard.
2. Rates were planned **per line** (`clamp(natural/slot, 1, 1.3)`), plus a catch-up boost of `1 + lag/dur` per line, so the rate jumped by 0.3 or more between lines.
3. Calibration was wall-clock based (it included the start latency), lasted only for the session, and covered one factor for all voices. A slower voice (Thomas) fell behind, and switching voices changed the speed.
4. Voice gender flipped on every line from one pitch reading: 165 Hz threshold, no hysteresis, no markers. A shouting man (F0 above 250 Hz) counted as female.

## What changed
| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `sync/fit.ts` | `planTrack(lines, input, opts)`. It groups lines into clusters (gap < 0.7 s), gives each cluster **one pace** so that its estimated speech fits from its start (up to 0.4 s early, only when needed and when the voice is free) to its end plus the tail. Overflow is projected into the next cluster instead of being skipped. Paces are smoothed (≤ 0.06 per line in the plan, only ever raised). Caps: `rateCap = min(1.3 × voiceSpeed, 1.35)`. Floor: 0.85 × voiceSpeed. Planning is done in "pace" space: rate = pace / voiceFactor, so every voice sounds equally fast. `VoiceCalibration`: per-voice EMA of the speed factor (the first samples converge fast, clamped to 0.6–1.6). `loadVoiceCal`/`saveVoiceCal` use `chrome.storage.local['voiceCal']`. The v1.0 `fit()` is unchanged (strict mode). |
| `sync/scheduler.ts` | Continuous mode was rewritten; strict mode is unchanged. It uses the whole-track plan (`planTrack` option, `replan()`). Lines chain back to back: the next line starts when the voice frees up if it is due within 0.7 s (`fillGapS`). Planned early starts. Lag controller: `extra += 0.35 × (clamp(lag/8, 0, 0.12) − extra)` per line. Rate limiter: \|Δrate\| ≤ 0.08 between lines of the same voice; across a voice switch it is the *pace* that is limited. A line is skipped only when lag > `maxLagMs` (the session passes 6 s). It then resyncs at a sentence that started ≤ 1.5 s ago, and the limiter state is kept. Pre-queue: with `Speaker.canQueue()`, the next line is handed to the engine 0.3 s before the estimated end of the current one, so the engine chains them with no gap. Seek, pause and stop handle the queued line too. Lines are never cut mid-sentence. |
| `voice/webspeech.ts` | Engine-level FIFO. All chunks of a line are queued in `speechSynthesis` at once, and a line played while another is speaking queues right behind it (no onend→speak gap). The watchdog guards the head utterance and is re-armed on `onstart`; it is suspended while paused. Stopping one line cancels and **re-queues** the other unfinished lines in order. `stopAll` cancels everything. `onMeasure` reports the onstart→onend speech time per line. `canQueue()`. |
| `sync/pitch.ts` | `SpeakerTracker`: confident male < 155 Hz, confident female > 205 Hz, anything between counts as no evidence. A voice switch happens only on: a caption label; a **speaker-change marker plus confident opposite evidence** (on that line or on the next confident one); or **2 adjacent confident opposite lines**, at least 6 s after the last switch. Shouting guard: a high F0 whose RMS is in this video's top 20 % counts as not confident. Frames now carry RMS. `speakerMarker(line)` reads `Line.newSpeaker`, or a `>>` / dialogue-dash prefix. `SpeakerPitch.observe(line, marker?)` and `evidence(line)` were added. |
| `session.ts` | It no longer drops late lines in continuous mode (only beyond 6 s). It passes `planTrack` with per-line voice factors: calibrated per voice key `engine:lang:voice`, or exact from `knownMs` for API/neural audio. The pace reference is the mean factor of the voices in use. Web Speech `onMeasure` and API `knownMs` feed the calibration, which triggers a replan; it is saved after 5 s (debounced) and on stop. Speaker evidence is read from every line whose original audio has fully played, right before the voice is chosen. A voice change triggers a replan. `canQueue` is forwarded from the engine. The wall-clock `calibrate()` was removed. New deps `loadVoiceCal`/`saveVoiceCal`. |
| `shared/types.ts` | Additive: `Line.newSpeaker?: boolean`. |

## Metrics: synthetic 10-min fast-talker track
`tests/unit/helpers/pace-sim.ts`: 211 lines of 1.2–4 s; gaps are 82 % 0.05–0.3 s and 18 % 0.7–1.3 s; speech density about 90 %. The French text is 20 % longer than the slots. The fake engine has a 0.12 s start latency and no latency when queued. "Silence" means silent time that overlaps caption speech.

| Scenario | spoken | max silence | rate max | max \|Δrate\| | max lag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Before** (v1.0), voice = prior | 100 % | 0.94 s | 1.32 | **0.31** | 0.33 s |
| **Before** (v1.0), voice 12 % slower than the prior, with the v1.0 session late-drop | **68 %** | **4.3 s** (67 silences > 1 s) | 1.49 | **0.32** | 0.42 s |
| After, voice = prior, Web Speech queue | 100 % | 0.42 s | 1.12 | 0.065 | 0.55 s |
| After, voice 12 % slower, live calibration | 100 % | 0.42 s | 1.28 | 0.080 | 0.76 s |
| After, same, engine without queue (audio) | 100 % | 0.34 s | 1.30 | 0.080 | 1.50 s |

Goals asserted in `s-pacing.test.ts` (4 scenarios × seeds): ≥ 95 % spoken, 0 cuts, silence ≤ 1.2 s, rate ≤ 1.35, \|Δrate\| ≤ 0.08, lag ≤ 3 s. All of them pass.

Stress runs (not asserted):
- French +35 %, calibrated: all goals still met (lag 0.46 s).
- Uncalibrated voice 15 % slow: 98 % spoken, but the mean lag reaches 3.2 s (the controller alone is gentle by design). Calibration removes this after a few lines.
- French +50–80 %: beyond the 1.3 cap, so 74–90 % spoken with clean resyncs and no cuts.

## Open issues / requests
1. **A (`captions.ts`): `mergeSentences` drops `newSpeaker`.** `Piece.newSpeaker` breaks the line but is not copied onto the `Line`. Please carry it, e.g. `cur = {…, newSpeaker: p.newSpeaker}` and in `flush` `if (c.newSpeaker) line.newSpeaker = true;`. Also check that `pipeline.ts` translation keeps the field. Until then, markers only come from `>>` / `- ` prefixes left in `text`/`source` (rare, because captions.ts strips them). So in practice voice switches now rely on the 2-adjacent-lines rule.
2. **P (neural/audio engines):** expose `canQueue()` plus gapless chaining if possible. Without it, audio engines still meet the goals in the sim, with about 0.12 s start gaps. `knownMs` from neural prepare is used for exact pacing and calibration automatically.
3. API/neural voices are bound at prefetch time (≤ 30 s ahead), so a voice switch reaches them late. A shorter prefetch horizon when gender matching is on would help (a cost/latency trade-off).
4. Real-Chrome checks are still to do: Chrome voices that never fire `onstart` (the watchdog still runs at the head, but there is no measurement then); queue behaviour of Google network voices with several pending utterances; Web Speech rate is not perfectly linear (calibration absorbs this per voice).
5. `setRate` on Web Speech applies only to re-queued chunks. A mid-line `ratechange` takes effect from the next line.
